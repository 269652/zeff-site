Local-first

It runs on your computer. Your data stays there.

Zeff is a program you download and start, not a service you sign up for. There is no Zeff cloud: the database is a file on your disk.

Your AI subscription

The AI steps run through the Claude Code, Codex or Copilot CLI you already use, signed in with your own account. Pick a model per task.

Nothing marked as read

Mail, WhatsApp, Signal and Teams are read from their lists. No chat is opened, so no blue ticks and no read receipts.

Data boundaries

Label any source or database open, personal or secret. Agents only see personal data when you allow it, and secret data never leaves its place.

On your phone too

Reach Zeff from your phone through Tailscale, your own private network. Behind a password, nothing open to the internet.

Encrypted backups

AES-256 encrypted, daily, with daily, weekly and monthly copies. Optional copy to your Google Drive. Restore in one click.

Several accounts

Keep work and private apart: each account has its own data, settings, AI backends and plugins.

Everything is a plugin

One place for what reaches you, what you owe, and what you know.

Turn on what you use. Each part works alone and gets better with the others.

Briefing and todos

Your tickets, mails and chats read by the AI each morning: what happened, what to do.

  • Todos with ready actions: a reply, a mail draft, a meeting in a free slot, a transfer
  • Open loops: what you promised and what was promised to you
  • Context just in time before a meeting or beside a mail
  • Repeating todos (a rate every 15th) that can't be skipped by mistake

Workspace

Pages and databases like Notion, wired into everything else in Zeff.

  • Block editor, page tree, backlinks, history and trash
  • Tables, boards, calendars, galleries, timelines and views you write yourself
  • Agents and automations on a schedule or an event
  • Templates: journal, finances, contracts, projects, meeting notes

People

A profile for everyone who matters, built from your messages.

  • One person across mail, WhatsApp, Signal and Teams
  • Birthdays and dates with reminders
  • Your rhythm with each person, and who you are drifting away from
  • Who owes whom

Money and paperwork

Letters, bills and cases in one place, read out of your documents.

  • Cases held together by their case numbers, with deadlines and instalments
  • Pay with a GiroCode or a Revolut link in one scan
  • Subscriptions you forgot, and your cashflow for the next 90 days
  • Documents: PDFs, scans, Office files and audio, read and searchable

Journal and insights

Write a line a day. Zeff writes the weekly and monthly review.

  • Mood, energy and your thoughts, one entry a day
  • Correlations computed in code: sleep, medication and its time, meetings against your mood
  • A timeline of everything that happened, across sources
  • Questions about your own data: “When did I last talk to Anna?”

Work and code

For people whose day runs on tickets.

  • Workers and live agent sessions on your todos, on their own branches
  • Merge requests, comments and delivery from the todo
  • Time tracking: jobs, weekly and monthly reports, booking suggestions
  • MCP tools so your agents can use Zeff

Questions
Does my data go to a Zeff server?

No. There is no Zeff server. Your data lives in a database file on your computer. The AI steps go to the AI provider of the CLI you chose, under your own account.

Do I need all the sources?

No. Every source and feature is a plugin you can switch on or off. Zeff with just a Workspace and a journal works fine.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes, as a web app: install Tailscale on your computer and your phone, set a password in Settings → Mobile access, and add Zeff to your home screen.

Which languages?

English, German and Spanish, for the interface and for dates and numbers.

What does “non-commercial” mean here?

Your own life and your own projects. If you use Zeff for paid work or inside a company, you need a commercial licence.

What happens to my data at 1.0?

It stays yours. Updating replaces the program, not your data, and you can export everything as backups at any time.
